# V031C.3 — Multidimensional Knowledge Inference

## Outcome

- Builds a rebuildable Knowledge snapshot from V031C.2 Company Signals.
- Separates observed Technology, Product, End Market, Capability, Infrastructure, and Supply-chain Role signals from inferred Theme, Sector, and Cluster classifications.
- Supports chained inference while retaining the originating signal IDs and Canonical Business Evidence IDs at every level.
- Rejects ticker and company membership fields in inference rules.

## Current population

- Registry records retained: 6,464
- Companies with inferred Knowledge classifications: 103
- Companies with signals but no classification under the current ontology: 111
- Inferred Theme records: 105
- Inferred Sector records: 57
- Inferred Cluster records: 6

These counts reflect the current 280-company Business Evidence population. They are not a fixed Research Universe and will grow automatically as Canonical Evidence and concept rules expand.

## Boundary

This version produces classifications and their evidence paths. News, ETF, supply-chain, and Deep Research eligibility decisions remain a downstream policy step and do not affect classification.
