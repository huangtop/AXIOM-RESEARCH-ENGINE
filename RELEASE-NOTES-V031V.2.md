# V031V.2 — Security Identity and Instrument Normalization

V031V.2 separates operating-company valuation scope from exchange-listed instruments. It preserves every Registry record while preventing warrant-, unit-, right-, and preferred-only shells from creating standalone seven-model company pages.

Classification uses official security names and exchange symbols, never a maintained ticker membership list. A company remains in valuation scope when at least one common or ordinary equity is linked; its non-common instruments are excluded individually. Instrument-only pseudo-companies are quarantined with machine-readable reasons.

Production results:

- 6,464 Registry company identities and 7,451 securities retained;
- 5,876 companies admitted to valuation scope;
- 588 instrument-only identities excluded;
- 475 warrants, 309 units, 131 subscription rights, and 477 preferred securities identified.

The full-market valuation service and market population now consume this scope. Excluded instruments remain auditable and can later be relinked to an underlying issuer when verified identity evidence becomes available.
