# V031C.4 — Research Eligibility Policy

## Outcome

- Separately decides whether each company qualifies for news screening, ETF monitoring, supply-chain analysis, and Deep Research.
- Consumes only V031C.3 Knowledge plus normalized security identity; valuation readiness is not an input.
- Requires both Theme and Sector evidence for the Research Universe, preventing generic mentions of AI from becoming core research membership.
- Applies an automatic evidence-based rank limit of 300 companies without a ticker membership list.

## Current evidence population

- Full Registry records retained: 6,464
- Evidence-qualified Research Universe: 21
- News screening enabled: 21
- ETF monitoring enabled: 17
- Supply-chain analysis enabled: 15
- Deep Research enabled: 2

The current cohort is intentionally smaller than the long-term 200–300 company target because Canonical Business Evidence currently covers only 280 companies. Eligibility will be rebuilt automatically as evidence coverage expands.

## Decision boundaries

- News requires material Theme and Sector evidence.
- ETF monitoring additionally requires normalized active common or ordinary equity.
- Supply-chain analysis additionally requires a credible Supply-chain Role or Cluster.
- Deep Research additionally requires strong Cluster evidence, multiple source signals, and at least three derived Knowledge dimensions.
- Every unavailable decision retains explicit unmet reason codes.
